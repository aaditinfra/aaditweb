FAQ

Common Questions

What is the typical timeline to complete a premium G+3 home?

Generally 12 to 15 months depending on design complexity (15 to 18 months for the Classical package). This includes architectural planning, civil structure and interior fit-outs.

Do you handle the entire construction process end-to-end?

Yes. We are a turnkey design-and-build firm, managing everything from soil testing and architectural design to civil construction, interiors and handover.

Can you integrate private elevators and home automation?

Yes. We specialise in vertical G+3 layouts, planning structural and electrical provisions early for lifts and smart-home systems.

Will Aadit Infra handle the BBMP/BDA plan sanctions?

Yes. Our team manages the liaisoning process, so your G+3 plan complies with Bengaluru building bylaws, with the necessary sanctions and Khata certificates secured.

How do you ensure the building complies with local zoning laws?

We verify your plot before design and design strictly within BBMP setbacks, FAR limits and road-width rules.

What quality control measures do you practise on site?

A multi-tier audit covering concrete slump tests, steel grade verification (Fe 550) and multi-layer waterproofing for roofs and bathrooms.

Do you offer a post-handover warranty?

Yes, a multi-year structural and waterproofing warranty. The exact term will be stated in your contract.
